Management HOW-TO

What this folder is for
- Central source for product & project documentation: PRD, project charter, meeting minutes, sprint reviews and milestone decisions.

Structure
- project-charter.md — initial scope, stakeholders, DARCI/RACI, high-level goals
- prd.md — product requirements, user flows, per-page usage, related links
- minutes-of-meetings/ — chronological meeting notes (dated files)
- sprint-reviews/ — post-sprint summaries, metrics, demos notes

How & when to fill
- Project kickoff: populate project-charter and PRD high-level sections.
- Each sprint: record sprint reviews and decisions; capture action items and owners in minutes-of-meetings.
- Major stakeholder review: update PRD and decision logs and increment version.

How to use (for PMs / Eng managers / stakeholders)
- PMs: keep PRD focused on business goals and acceptance criteria; link to designs and TRDs.
- Stakeholders: check minutes for decisions and actions assigned to them.
- Eng leads: use sprint-reviews to extract engineering metrics and blockers.

Maintenance
- Add version and last-updated at top of PRD and Charter.
- Tag minutes with tags (e.g., "decision", "action", "risk") and maintain an actions tracker in PRD or separate spreadsheet.
- Archive older sprint review artifacts yearly or when superseded.

Templates & quick rules
- Meeting minutes: Date | Attendees | Decisions | Action items (owner + due)
- Sprint review: Sprint # | Summary | Demos | Metrics | Blockers | Next steps

Contact
- Project Manager is the primary owner for management docs; assign backups for continuity.
